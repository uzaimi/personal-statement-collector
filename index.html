PERSONAL WINDOWS UTILITY

Personal Statement Collector

Organize credit-card statements and view statement balances, minimum payments and due dates by month.

How it works

  1. The owner connects their Gmail account using Google's authorization screen.
  2. When manually launched on their Windows PC, the utility finds bank statement emails and downloads PDF attachments.
  3. It reads the PDFs locally, using local OCR when needed, and saves monthly balance reports on the PC.

Gmail access

The utility requests Gmail read-only access to find statement messages and download their attachments. Google grants this permission across the mailbox; the utility filters for the configured bank senders. It does not send, modify or delete email.

Personal use

This is a private utility operated by its owner, not a public financial service. This website provides information only and does not provide Gmail sign-in or accept statement uploads. The utility runs manually and does not require ChatGPT.

Reports show original statement amounts. They do not verify later payments or initiate payments. Unclear statement readings are flagged for review.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. For app questions, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile; do not post financial data or credentials publicly.
