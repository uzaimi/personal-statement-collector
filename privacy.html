Privacy policy

Effective 19 September 2026. This policy describes the personal Windows utility Personal Statement Collector, maintained by uzaimi, and this informational website.

Information accessed

With the owner's consent, the utility uses the Gmail API with the gmail.readonly scope. It searches configured bank statement senders and retrieves matching email messages, headers and PDF attachments. Extracted information includes bank names, card endings, statement dates, due dates, balances and minimum payments.

Purpose and processing

Information is used only to download, organize and summarize the owner's statements, prevent duplicate processing and identify items needing review. PDF extraction and OCR run locally on the owner's PC. The standalone utility does not send statement content to OpenAI or a cloud OCR service.

Storage and protection

Downloaded PDFs, extracted records, reports and diagnostic information are stored locally. Gmail authorization tokens and configured PDF passwords are encrypted using Windows protection tied to the user's account. Reports and downloaded files are not additionally encrypted by the utility and depend on the PC's security. Original PDF encryption is retained when present. Local test artifacts may include cached emails, page images and extracted text.

Sharing and external services

The utility communicates with Google to authorize access and retrieve Gmail data. It does not sell information, use it for advertising, or automatically upload statements or reports to this website or GitHub. Any separate backups, cloud-folder synchronization or manual sharing configured by the owner are outside the utility's control.

Personal Statement Collector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local statements and reports remain until the owner deletes them; there is no automatic retention deadline. The owner can delete downloaded PDFs, reports, registers, test artifacts and local credentials from the collector's folders. Revoke the app's access through Google Account connections to prevent further Gmail access. Revoking access does not delete previously downloaded files, and deleting local files does not delete email from Gmail.

This website

This static site contains no app-added analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No Gmail data is needed to visit it.

Contact and changes

Contact the maintainer through the uzaimi GitHub profile. Do not share passwords, tokens or statements in public issues. Policy updates will be published here with a revised effective date.
