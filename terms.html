Purpose

Personal Statement Collector is a personal Windows utility for organizing statements from an account the operator owns or is authorized to access. This website describes the utility; it does not offer a hosted financial service.

Review financial information

PDF formats and OCR results can change. Verify report amounts and due dates against original statements and the bank's current records before acting. Reports contain original statement balances and do not establish amounts still unpaid after subsequent payments or transfers. The utility does not provide financial advice or make payments.

The operator is responsible for protecting their PC, credentials and saved reports, and for complying with applicable Google and GitHub terms. The utility requires internet access for Gmail and may need renewed authorization or maintenance when bank formats or service requirements change.
